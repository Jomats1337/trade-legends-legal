2. Information we collect

  • Account information: your account identifier, display name, selected avatar and, if you register, email address. We store password hashes and recovery-code verification data rather than plaintext passwords or recovery codes.
  • Optional Google sign-in: if you choose Google sign-in through Firebase Authentication, we process your Firebase user identifier (UID), verified email address, display name and authentication tokens to verify your identity and link it to your game account. We store the UID and email for that link; the game service does not copy your Google display name or profile photo into your game profile. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Gameplay and preferences: virtual balances, simulated trades and transaction history, competition results, rankings, achievements, rewards, referrals, mining progress, settings, language and notification choices. Guest play also creates a server-side profile.
  • Service and security information: sessions, request identifiers, timestamps, service activity and error information. Our infrastructure processes network information such as IP addresses to deliver requests and protect the service.
  • Installation measurement, where enabled: a random installation identifier, app/package and version information, Google Play install-referrer information (which may include campaign and click identifiers), referral-click and install timestamps, and collection time. Our installation identifier is separate from your game account and is not a hardware identifier. This collector does not send your account email, password, balance or session token.
  • App use and advertising measurement: installation and campaign identifiers, first-install and app-opening times, app version, and details of ad impressions such as their value, currency, measurement precision, format, placement, response identifier and advertising network. We associate these records to measure app usage, retention and advertising performance. These cohort records do not include your account email or game balance. Any revenue figures here are developer advertising revenue, not virtual currency, and not your bank or payment information.
  • App analytics and diagnostics: Google Analytics for Firebase processes app interactions, screen views, sessions, installation identifiers, app/device information and approximate location derived from network information. Firebase Crashlytics processes crash reports, stack traces, diagnostic app/device information and installation identifiers. We do not attach account emails, passwords, balances or raw installation-referrer values to custom analytics events or crash reports. Google Analytics for Firebase does not collect the advertising ID, and its advertising-personalization signals are disabled.
  • Advertising: Advertising technologies used in Trade Legends may collect your IP address and approximate location inferred from it, advertising and other device identifiers, device and network information, interactions with ads such as impressions, clicks and viewability, and technical information such as diagnostics, crash logs and other app performance data. These data support ad delivery and measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. We do not request precise device location for advertising or provide your age or gender to these advertising SDKs. Choices you make in the consent message are stored on your device.
  • Remote notifications: Firebase Cloud Messaging processes installation identifiers and notification tokens to deliver messages. Notification delivery and interactions may also be measured through Firebase Analytics.
  • Communications: your contact details and information you choose to send us when requesting support or exercising your privacy rights. If you choose to send optional freeform feedback comments or ratings, these are stored on the connected game server and are not published as a public Google Play review.
  • Local device storage: session credentials, cached progress, preferences and installation-delivery state are kept in app storage so the app can operate.

Trade Legends does not require access to your contacts, microphone, camera or precise device location. It does not request payment-card details or cryptocurrency private keys. All in-game funds are virtual.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep account information and gameplay records while needed to provide your account and its history, unless you request deletion earlier. After verified account deletion, account credentials, sessions, personal profile, account-linked activity and progress are removed from the active game service. Shared competition or transaction records may retain de-identified entries needed to preserve other players’ results.

Installation measurement is stored separately from game accounts and is subject to the attribution service’s retention schedule. It is not automatically identifiable from an account email. If you want installation records removed too, include that request when contacting us; we may need additional non-secret information to locate them. Uninstalling the app, clearing its storage or deleting a game account does not by itself prove that separate installation records have been removed. The separate app-use and advertising measurement records described in “Information we collect” are kept in their own storage and are not automatically deleted when you delete your game account.

Firebase analytics, diagnostic and messaging records are separate from your game account. Their retention and deletion depend on the Firebase product and configured retention settings; aggregated analytics reports may remain after event-level data expires. Crashlytics retains crash reports for 90 days before deletion from live and backup systems begins. Cloud Messaging installation identifiers are retained until deletion is requested, after which Google describes removal from live and backup systems within 180 days. Deleting your game account or uninstalling the app does not automatically delete all Firebase records. When you delete a game account linked to Google sign-in, the app asks Firebase Authentication to delete the corresponding Firebase sign-in user. That request is sent from the app and may be interrupted; if it does not finish, you may need a network connection, to relaunch the app, or to sign in again to complete it. This does not delete your Google account. Contact us to request deletion of these records; we may need additional non-secret installation information to identify them, and will explain any identification or deletion limits. The linked Firebase privacy documentation provides product-specific details.

Advertising partners retain information according to their own policies and the purposes for which it is processed, including advertising measurement, fraud prevention and legal obligations. We do not set one common retention period for every advertising SDK. See each partner's notice for its retention criteria and options to request access or deletion. Removing the app or resetting an advertising identifier does not necessarily delete information previously received by a partner.

We retain support communications, security records and restricted backup copies only as long as reasonably necessary for their purpose, recovery, dispute handling or an applicable legal requirement. Retention depends on the record’s purpose, whether an issue remains open, and applicable obligations. Backup data is not used for routine gameplay and is removed through backup rotation. A deletion request may therefore take additional time to be reflected in backup copies. If an exception requires retaining information, we limit its use to that purpose and explain the reason where legally permitted.

Read the account and data deletion instructions to make a request without reinstalling the app.
